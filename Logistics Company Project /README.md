## Hello,So, what is FleetOps?

Imagine you're managing a logistics company.

Your trucks are moving.  
Deliveries are happening.  
Revenue is coming in.

Everything *looks* fine.

But somewhere in the background:
- late deliveries are quietly stacking up,
- maintenance costs are rising,
- inefficiencies are bleeding profit,
- and no one can clearly tell *where the money is leaking.*

That’s the problem **FleetOps** was built to solve.

This project is an **integrated logistics and profitability dashboard** designed to connect operational performance with what really matters:

**business impact.**

Not just *what happened* — but **what it cost.**

# Why this dataset?

Because it felt honest.

Logistics data is messy.

And I mean *really* messy.

It’s:
- incomplete timestamps,
- overlapping maintenance logs,
- delivery delays with unclear causes,
- and operational records that don’t always cooperate.

Which is exactly why I chose it.

Because that’s real life.

Real analytics isn’t about perfectly clean CSV files.

It’s about taking messy systems and turning them into useful decisions.

---

# The moment everything changed: “The 1.11 Problem”

At one point in development, I created a delivery performance KPI.

It showed this:

`1.11`

Mathematically correct.

Terrible dashboard.

If you’re an executive and you see **1.11**, your first thought is:

*"What am I looking at?"*

That was the moment I learned something important:

> Accuracy alone doesn’t create clarity.

So I pivoted.

Instead of displaying average delay, I reframed the same metric into:

## 95.58% On-Time Reliability

Suddenly everything clicked.

It became understandable.
It became actionable.
It became useful.

That one change completely changed the story the dashboard was telling.

And honestly—that was one of my biggest lessons in this project.

---

# What this project taught me

### 1. Context beats data
Numbers alone don’t solve problems.

Context does.

For example:

Instead of dumping maintenance costs into a table, I built a **High-Impact Maintenance Drivers Funnel**.

Now the insight becomes immediate:

 Brake repairs and engine issues are the biggest cost drivers.

That tells a manager exactly where to act.

That’s the difference.

---

### 2. Design is part of the analysis
A dashboard isn’t just information.

It’s communication.

I experimented with a design style I’d call:

**Minimalism with Pop**

Clean layout.  
Minimal clutter.  
Electric cobalt highlights.

The goal:

Make the important numbers impossible to miss.

---

### 3. Small details matter
One visual originally showed:

`0.83`

Technically fine.

But changing it to:

`83%`

made it instantly understandable.

That tiny formatting change taught me something:

Good dashboards respect the user’s attention.

# What FleetOps tracks

This dashboard gives visibility into:

× Net Operating Margin  
× On-Time Delivery Reliability  
× Fleet Capacity Utilization  
× Maintenance Cost Drivers  
× Delivery Delay Trends  
× Operational Risk & Incidents  

In simple terms:

**How healthy is the fleet—and what is that doing to the business?**
