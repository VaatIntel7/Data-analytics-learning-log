# Power BI Project Milestone: Sales Data Visualization Dashboard

## Project Overview
As part of my ongoing data analytics journey, I developed this **Power BI dashboard** to transform raw sales data into clear, actionable business insights.

The objective of this project was more than just building charts—it was about understanding the story hidden in the data and presenting it in a way that supports better decision-making.

Through this project, I focused on answering key business questions such as:

- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Who are the most valuable customers?
- What patterns and opportunities can be uncovered from the sales data?

This exercise strengthened both my **technical Power BI skills** and my ability to think critically like a data analyst—moving from raw numbers to meaningful insights.

---

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Preparation & Modeling
Every strong dashboard starts with clean data.

Before building the visuals, I:
- cleaned and structured the raw sales dataset,
- corrected inconsistencies,
- and prepared the data model to ensure accurate reporting and reliable analysis.

This foundation was essential for creating trustworthy visual outputs.

---

### 2. Dashboard Development
The dashboard was designed to focus on two core performance areas:

#### Customer Performance Analysis
I created a **column chart** to visualize **Total Sales by Customer Name**, making it easier to identify:
- top-performing customers,
- high-value buyers,
- and potential targets for retention strategies.

#### Product Performance Analysis
To evaluate product contribution, I built a **horizontal bar chart** showing **Sales by Product Sub-Category**.

This allowed for quick comparison and clearly highlighted which products drive the most revenue.

---

### 3. Dashboard Design Approach
Beyond functionality, I prioritized user experience.

My design approach focused on:
- simplicity,
- clarity,
- and consistent visual formatting.

The goal was to ensure that the **data remained the focal point**, allowing insights to stand out without unnecessary visual distractions.

---

## Key Insights Discovered

After analyzing the dashboard, several important findings became clear:

- **Phones** and **Chairs** emerged as the strongest revenue-generating product categories, significantly outperforming other sub-categories.
- Lower-performing categories such as **Bookcases** and **Furnishings** may require strategic review or targeted improvement.
- Customer spending patterns varied noticeably, revealing opportunities for **customer segmentation**, loyalty initiatives, and personalized engagement strategies.
